# Pricing model

I quote in three tiers: a minimal fixed-scope tier, a standard tier (what I
expect them to take), and an everything tier that makes the standard look
reasonable. The middle tier carries the real scope; the outer two exist to
frame it.

**Working hypothesis — one supporting data point:** clients who need to bring
others into a decision may respond well to a clear middle tier and a written
proposal they can forward. On the 7/21 call, [[jordan-at-acme]] reacted well
to the middle tier, asked for the proposal in writing, and said they would
take it to their CEO before procurement. This is a hypothesis to test, not a
pattern yet.

What to test next: whether the minimal tier attracts clients I shouldn't
take. Revisit after two more proposals.
