# Client call — Acme site relaunch kickoff

Call with [[jordan-at-acme]], my main contact at Acme, who runs the marketing
team and owns the relaunch budget, about 40 minutes.

- Jordan confirmed budget approval for the relaunch — "we finally got the
  sign-off Tuesday." Wants to launch before their October trade show.
- Scope, in their words: new marketing site, keep the existing store as-is,
  migrate the blog "only if it's cheap."
- Jordan flagged that their CEO hates carousels. Wrote that down twice.
- I quoted the standard three-tier structure from my [[pricing-model]]: a
  minimal fixed-scope tier, a standard tier — the one I expect clients to
  take — carrying the real scope, and an everything tier, with the outer two
  framing the standard. Jordan reacted well to the middle tier and asked for
  the proposal in writing by Friday.
- Decision on the call: I send the tier-two proposal; Jordan pre-socializes
  it with the CEO before it goes to procurement.
